# Introduction

Wallpy is a dynamic wallpaper engine that automatically changes your desktop wallpaper based on time of day and location. It's designed to provide a seamless experience that matches your local environment, creating a more immersive desktop experience.

## Key Features

- **Time-based Changes**: Automatically updates wallpapers based on time of day and solar events
- **Location Awareness**: Adjusts timing based on your local sunrise and sunset times
- **Custom Packs**: Create and share your own wallpaper packs with the community
- **Easy Installation**: Simple one-command installation and setup process
- **Gallery Access**: Browse and download from a growing collection of wallpaper packs
- **Cross-platform**: Works on Windows, with macOS and Linux support coming soon

## How It Works

Wallpy uses your system's location and time to determine the appropriate wallpaper to display. It supports four main time periods:

1. **Sunrise**: Early morning wallpapers
2. **Day**: Daytime wallpapers
3. **Sunset**: Evening wallpapers
4. **Night**: Nighttime wallpapers

The application runs as a background service, ensuring smooth transitions between wallpapers without any user intervention.
